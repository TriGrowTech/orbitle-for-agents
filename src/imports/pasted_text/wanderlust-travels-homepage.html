How do I book a package on Wanderlust? ▼
Simply search for your destination, browse available packages, and click "Book Now" or "Enquire." You can also fill out our Plan a Tour form and a dedicated expert will reach out within 2 hours with a custom quote.
Are all travel agents on this platform verified? ▼
Yes, every agent undergoes a thorough verification process including identity checks, travel license verification, and a background check before being approved on the platform.
Can I customise an existing package? ▼
Absolutely! Every package can be customised. Just click "Customise This Trip" on any package page and tell us your preferences — hotels, activities, travel dates, dietary needs — and the agent will adapt it.
What is your cancellation and refund policy? ▼
Cancellation policies vary by package. Most packages offer full refunds up to 30 days before departure, partial refunds between 30–15 days, and no refund within 15 days. Always check the specific policy on the package detail page.
Is it safe to pay online through your platform? ▼
Yes. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways. We support UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and EMI options. Your card details are never stored on our servers.
Do packages include flights? ▼
This varies by package. Each package clearly states what's included in the Inclusions section on the detail page. Some packages offer optional flight add-ons or you can request a custom package with flights.
How can I become a travel agent on Wanderlust? ▼
Click "Become an Agent" in the top bar or footer. You'll be directed to our Orbitle for Agents platform where you can register, submit documents, and start listing packages within 24 hours of approval.

📖 About This Package

Experience the magic of Bali with your partner in a private infinity-pool villa. This meticulously crafted romance package blends Ubud's spiritual serenity with Seminyak's beachside luxury. Every detail — from candle-lit dinners to couple's spa rituals — is thoughtfully arranged so you can focus on creating memories.

🗓 Day-by-Day Itinerary

1
Arrival & Villa Check-In

Airport welcome, transfer to private villa. Evening: sunset cocktails at Tanah Lot Temple with personal butler service.

2
Ubud Spiritual Journey

Tegalalang Rice Terrace walk, Tirta Empul Temple blessing, couples' Balinese massage at a cliffside spa, candlelit jungle dinner.

3
Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Pre-dawn guided trek to Mount Batur's crater for a breathtaking sunrise. Return for champagne breakfast at your villa.

4
Beach & Watersports

Full day at Nusa Dua beach — snorkelling, jet-ski, sunset parasailing. Private beachside dinner under the stars.

5
Seminyak Leisure Day

Shopping at Seminyak boutiques, La Lucciola beach club afternoon, rooftop cocktails at Ku De Ta at sunset.

6
Nusa Penida Island Day Trip

Speed boat to Nusa Penida, Kelingking Beach cliff hike, Angel's Billabong natural pool, return for farewell dinner.

7
Departure Day

Leisurely breakfast at your villa, last-minute shopping, airport transfer. Safe travels!
